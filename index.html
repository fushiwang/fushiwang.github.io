About me

Hello! I’m a BA/MS student at Boston University studying Mathematics, Economics, and Philosophy!

My research interests are in economic theory, modeling, and econometrics. Most recently, I’ve studied how the prospect of attracting attention might change a political candidate’s platform, by extending the Hotelling-Downs model using probabilistic parameters that change the perception of information. And, how communities recover from storms, using link county-coded NOAA storm events from 2000–2005 to CDC Social Vulnerability Index measures and county economic outcomes!

Alongside economics and mathematics, I also studied philosophy during my undergrad, enjoy playing the piano and Guzheng, and play badminton competitively!
