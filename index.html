About me

I’m a BA/MS student at Boston University. What draws me to economics is the distance between a few assumptions written on a page and the behavior they can explain. I especially enjoy the point where a theoretical question becomes one I can investigate with data.

My interests are in economic theory, probability, and econometrics. I’ve studied how the prospect of attracting attention might change a political candidate’s platform, and how communities recover from storms. In both projects, the part I find most interesting is deciding which observations would let us distinguish one explanation from another.

Alongside economics and mathematics, I study philosophy and play classical piano.
