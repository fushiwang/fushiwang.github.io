Fushi Wang

I’m a BA/MS student at Boston University studying economics and mathematics. My research interests are in economic theory and econometrics, especially how we model uncertainty and compare statistical models.

I work with James Feigenbaum on empirical research in economic history and applied microeconomics, and I teach undergraduate mathematics discussion sections.

Research

Dispersion-Weighted Expectation: A Probability Reweighting Framework

In development

How does an expectation change when a few unlikely outcomes dominate its value? I study a weighting rule that depends on each probability’s distance from the mean probability mass.

Standardized Bias–Variance Decomposition for Linear Model Selection

In development

I put bias and variance on a common scale and use simulations to compare the resulting model-selection rule with established information criteria.

County Disaster Vulnerability and Economic Recovery

May 2026

I use U.S. county data from 2000–2005 to examine how social vulnerability relates to storm damage and subsequent changes in unemployment.

Displacement of Alcohol Consumption by Cannabis Policy

With Ajay Raman · April 2026

We combine state cannabis policy measures with beverage-specific consumption data to study how beer, wine, and spirits consumption changes as states implement new policies.

State alcohol consumption, 1994–2023

50 states and Washington, D.C. · Annual observations

This extract from the cannabis policy project contains apparent consumption of beer, wine, spirits, and all beverages, measured in gallons of ethanol per capita. The source is NIAAA’s apparent alcohol consumption series, Table 4.

The R example plots the unweighted mean across states and D.C. for each beverage. Save both files in one folder and run Rscript alcohol-trends.R to generate the figure.
